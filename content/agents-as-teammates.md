When AI agents stop being prompted assistants and start being collaborators with agency, memory, and opinions of their own — almost everything about how organizations work has to change. Most of them aren't ready. Most of their tooling isn't either.

The companies that figure this out first won't have an *advantage*. They'll have a *compounding* one.
