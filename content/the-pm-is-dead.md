Something broke in the last six months. Not broke in a bad way — broke like a dam breaks. Everything that was building up behind it is now rushing through.

Two years ago, I managed products. The job was *negotiation* — between engineering and design, between roadmap and reality, between what the customer said they wanted and what they would actually use. I wrote documents. I ran meetings. I built consensus. I shipped features.

Today, the job is *orchestration*. Across humans **and** AI agents. Simultaneously.

> The title is the same. The job is unrecognizable.
